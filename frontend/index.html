End-to-End Prediction Architecture

How sensor telemetry transforms into physiological multi-horizon projections through dual-branch recurrence, self-attention, and homeostatic clearance.

01

Sensor Stream Ingestion

Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) sensor streams interstitial glucose levels paired with autonomic heart rate (HR) telemetry sampled at 5-minute intervals.

Sampling Rate: 5 min (±30s)
Required Field: GlucoseCGM (mg/dL)
Autonomic: HR (Median: 75 bpm)
↓ Continuous 60-min window chunking
02

Temporal Sanitization & Gap Rejection

Sliding window extracts the last 12 readings (60 minutes). Gaps between consecutive readings are checked: any sequence crossing sensor dropouts or disconnects (>5 min) is flagged and rejected to eliminate artificial delta spikes.

Window Size: 12 readings
Max Permitted Gap: 5 minutes
Filter: Glucose > 0 mg/dL
↓ Harmonic projection
03

Circadian Harmonic Transformation

Converts timestamps into 24-hour cyclical harmonic trigonometric features. Preserves continuity across midnight (23:59 to 00:01) to capture daily diurnal insulin sensitivity variations (Dawn phenomenon).

Trig Function: sin(2πm/1440)
Orthogonal: cos(2πm/1440)
Feature Vector: 4 dimensions
↓ Dual-branch tensor encoding
04

Dual-Branch Bi-LSTM + Transformer Network

The 4-feature temporal tensor is projected into a 64-dimensional hidden space and split across two specialized neural network branches:

Branch A: Bi-LSTM
Bidirectional Recurrence
Captures forward-backward velocity and acute acceleration across the 60-min window (128 features).
Branch B: Transformer
Self-Attention Heads (x4)
Computes multi-head attention over time-steps, weighing inflection points regardless of sequential distance (64 features).
Bi-LSTM Size: 2 x 64 = 128-d
Attention Heads: 4 (d_model=64)
Fusion MLP: 192 → 64 → 32 → 1
↓ Homeostatic clearance & momentum update
05

Physiological Clearance & Rollout Engine

Eliminates runaway autoregressive feedback drift. At each 5-minute forecast step forward, circadian time advances, momentum decays (γ = 0.82), and homeostatic clearance pulls toward baseline (115 mg/dL) to generate natural postprandial peaks and recovery dips.

Decay Factor (γ): 0.82 / 5-min
Restorative (k): 0.038
Target Equilibrium: 115.0 mg/dL
↓ Multi-horizon stratification
06

Clinical Horizon Triage & Patient Alerting

Produces synchronized trajectories across 30, 60, and 120-minute horizons. Applies Clarke Error Grid clinical safety thresholds to issue immediate proactive warnings for impending hypoglycemic episodes (<70 mg/dL) or postprandial spikes (>180 mg/dL).

Horizons: +30m, +60m, +120m
Hypoglycemia: < 70 mg/dL (Warning)
Hyperglycemia: > 180 mg/dL (Caution)
